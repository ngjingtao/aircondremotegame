# Scene script — what each image must show

Forty-eight plates, six per lesson, then twenty-three tiles. Use the prompt
lines in `PROMPTS.md`; use this file to judge whether a generated candidate is
actually teaching the right thing.

Paths are relative to `www/art/lessons/<lesson>/`.

## Electricity — `electric`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Battery | `01.webp` | One battery on a wooden workbench; two metal terminals; a soft charge glow. No wires yet. | Do not show a closed circuit or a lit bulb. |
| 2 Closed loop | `02.webp` | The same battery wired in a complete loop to a small open switch and a bulb; the path is clear. | The switch is **open**; bulb dark. |
| 3 Switch | `03.webp` | Close-up of the switch lever closing the loop; a tiny clean spark; the wire path continuous. | Not a broken wire; not an explosion. |
| 4 Lit bulb | `04.webp` | The same circuit with the bulb glowing warm; filament visible; soft light on the bench. | Do not change the room or the battery. |
| 5 Conductors | `05.webp` | A tray: copper coin + iron nail beside a rubber eraser + plastic ruler. The metal path glows; the plastic path is blocked. | No labels; do not make both glow. |
| 6 Safe socket | `06.webp` | A wall socket with a red warning triangle, a hand and a key pulled back, a red danger arc, safe distance. | Danger averted; no injury, no fire. |

## Magnetism — `magnet`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Attraction | `01.webp` | A bar magnet on a shelf pulling a nail and paperclips; a wooden block, eraser and glass cup stay still. | Only the magnetic things move. |
| 2 Poles | `02.webp` | Close-up of the magnet's two poles with soft field rings; **N** and **S** painted on the magnet. | N/S only; no other text. |
| 3 Pairs | `03.webp` | Two magnet pairs: N-to-N pushing apart, N-to-S snapping together; clear motion arcs. | Do not mix up which pair repels. |
| 4 Field lines | `04.webp` | Iron filings forming curved field lines around a bar magnet on paper. | Lines flow pole to pole, not random. |
| 5 Chain | `05.webp` | A nail touching a magnet, with a chain of paperclips hanging from the nail. | The chain hangs from the nail, not the magnet. |
| 6 Compass | `06.webp` | A compass on a shelf, needle pointing north, a soft curved Earth horizon behind; N/S on the compass. | No map labels or degrees. |

## Light — `light`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sources | `01.webp` | A warm lamp in a dim room, straight light rays, a watching eye in the foreground. | The eye is an observer, not a light source. |
| 2 Straight | `02.webp` | A beam passing through a slit in a card and landing as a straight line on a screen. | The beam must not bend. |
| 3 Shadow | `03.webp` | A small tree and a ball casting soft shadows on the floor in lamp light. | Shadows fall away from the lamp. |
| 4 Closer | `04.webp` | The lamp moved close to the ball, a much bigger shadow. | Same room, same ball, same lamp. |
| 5 Mirror | `05.webp` | A hand mirror on a stand reflecting a beam across the room. | Reflection angle looks believable. |
| 6 Refraction | `06.webp` | A glass of water with a straw that looks bent at the water line. | The straw is not actually broken. |

| 6 Protection | `06.webp` | The bear behind a green shield with a paw print and small hearts, the forest safe behind. | Hopeful, not sad; no traps or guns. |

## Speaker — `speaker`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cutaway | `01.webp` | A cutaway speaker cabinet showing the cone, magnet and coil. | The cabinet is cut open, not broken. |
| 2 Motor | `02.webp` | Close-up of the magnet and voice coil inside the speaker. | Coil sits in the magnet gap. |
| 3 Signal | `03.webp` | A soft signal waveform entering the coil, the coil pushing, motion arrows. | No Hz or numbers. |
| 4 Cone | `04.webp` | The cone pushing air, sound arcs and tiny dust particles moving. | Air moves both ways; not a wind tunnel. |
| 5 Pitch | `05.webp` | Dense high-frequency waves, the cone moving quickly with tight arcs. | Higher pitch ≠ louder. |
| 6 Volume | `06.webp` | Tall amplitude waves, a big sound arc, the cone at full excursion. | Louder ≠ higher pitch. |

## Power journey — `power`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Plant | `01.webp` | A power plant with a spinning turbine and generator, soft smoke, green landscape. | Not a nuclear cooling tower stereotype; keep it friendly. |
| 2 Step-up | `02.webp` | A step-up transformer beside the plant, coils and a tall bushing. | No voltage numbers. |
| 3 Grid | `03.webp` | Pylons carrying wires across hills, a soft current glow along the cables. | Wires connect, not float. |
| 4 Substation | `04.webp` | A fenced substation with transformers and insulators. | No danger to people; no climbing. |
| 5 Meter | `05.webp` | A distribution wire to a house, a meter on the wall. | No digits on the meter. |
| 6 Socket | `06.webp` | A wall socket and plug close-up with a small lightning motif. | No 230 V / 50 Hz text; the panel has it. |

## Electrical safety — `safety`

| Step | File | Must show | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Socket | `01.webp` | A wall socket with a key and a finger pulled away by a red warning arc. | No injury; danger averted. |
| 2 Water | `02.webp` | A dripping tap above a plug lying in a puddle; water droplets; danger. | The plug is not plugged in. |
| 3 Overload | `03.webp` | An extension lead with too many plugs, heat squiggles and a red glow. | No fire yet; warning moment. |

| 5 Earth/RCD | `05.webp` | A three-pin plug with the earth wire going to a ground symbol, an RCD box beside it. | Earth = green/yellow; no other text. |
| 6 Storm | `06.webp` | A stormy room with lightning, a short-circuit spark and smoke, a child safely back. | Fear is okay, injury is not. |
